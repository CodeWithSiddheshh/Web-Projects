Our Story

A decade of considered design, honest materials and quiet craft.

Founded in Mumbai, 2016

Designed with intention

VELORA began with a single question: what would a wardrobe look like if every piece earned its place? We started with one perfect white shirt and a commitment — nothing leaves the atelier until it is right.

Ten years later, that philosophy still governs everything we make. Small collections. Natural fibres. Construction that outlasts trends. We release fewer pieces each season and stand behind every one of them.

Our Philosophy

Fewer, better things

We reject the cycle of seasonal churn. Each VELORA piece is designed to be worn hundreds of times — to soften, fade and adapt to the life of its owner. We call this patina, and we design for it deliberately.

Our collections are built on a neutral palette and timeless proportions, so every piece works with what you already own.

Craftsmanship

Finished by hand

Every seam is checked, every button is corozo or horn, every hem is finished the slow way. We partner with 120 artisan families across India who have practised their craft for generations.

Each garment passes through 47 pairs of hands before it reaches yours — and it shows.

Sustainability

Responsibility, woven in

98% of our fibres are natural and certified. Our packaging is recycled and recyclable, our dyehouse recycles 90% of its water, and our repair programme keeps VELORA pieces in circulation for years longer.

We publish our supply chain annually — because trust is earned in the open.

The Journey

Our Approach, Year by Year

2016

The first shirt

VELORA launches with a single garment — the Essential Oxford — from a two-person studio in Fort, Mumbai.

2018

The atelier opens

We open our Kala Ghoda flagship and establish direct partnerships with artisan weavers in Tamil Nadu.

2021

Natural fibres, only

We commit to 100% natural and certified fibres across the entire collection — no exceptions, no blends with synthetics.

2024

Repair for life

Every VELORA piece gains a lifetime repair guarantee. Wear it, mend it, pass it on.

2026

Today

A community of 40,000 members across 30 countries — and we are still asking the same question from day one.
