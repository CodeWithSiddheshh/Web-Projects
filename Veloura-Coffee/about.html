Our Story

A journey of passion, craftsmanship, and the pursuit of the perfect cup

From Humble Beginnings

Veloura Coffee was born in 2015 from a simple yet powerful idea: that every cup of coffee should be an extraordinary experience. What started as a small coffee cart in the heart of New York has grown into a beloved destination for coffee enthusiasts.

Our founder, Marcus Chen, spent years traveling the world's coffee-growing regions, building relationships with farmers and learning the art of coffee cultivation. These relationships continue to form the foundation of our commitment to quality and sustainability.

Today, Veloura Coffee stands as a testament to the belief that great coffee is both an art and a science. Our master roasters and skilled baristas work in harmony to bring you beverages that delight the senses and warm the soul.

Our Mission & Values

Guided by principles that put quality, sustainability, and community at the heart of everything we do

Quality First

We source only the finest Arabica beans, carefully selecting each lot for its unique characteristics and flavor profile. Our rigorous quality standards ensure every cup meets our exacting expectations.

Sustainability

We partner directly with farmers who share our commitment to sustainable practices. Fair prices, environmental stewardship, and community development are integral to our sourcing philosophy.

Community

Coffee has a unique ability to bring people together. We strive to create welcoming spaces where connections are forged, ideas are shared, and moments are savored.

Craftsmanship

From roasting to brewing, every step is executed with precision and care. Our baristas undergo extensive training to master the art and science of coffee preparation.

Innovation

While we honor traditional methods, we continuously explore new techniques and flavors. Our innovative approach keeps the coffee experience fresh and exciting.

Integrity

Transparency and honesty guide our relationships with customers, partners, and employees. We believe in doing the right thing, even when no one is watching.

The Veloura Experience

At Veloura Coffee, we believe that coffee is more than just a beverage—it's a ritual, a moment of pause in a busy day, an opportunity to connect with others or simply be present with yourself.

Our cafes are designed to be sanctuaries where the aroma of freshly roasted beans mingles with the gentle hum of conversation and the soft whir of espresso machines. Every detail, from the warm lighting to the comfortable seating, is carefully considered to enhance your experience.

Whether you're grabbing a quick morning espresso or settling in for an afternoon of work, we invite you to make Veloura Coffee your home away from home.
